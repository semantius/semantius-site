The Semantius app is a metadata-driven web interface for viewing and managing
your data. Generated directly from your [data catalog](/docs/data-catalog), it
gives teams a visual alternative to the agent-optimized
[skills](/docs/agent-skills) and [CLI](/docs/cli).

<Image src={app1} alt="The Semantius app showing the Customers list of a sample organization, with search, sort, filter and pagination controls" class="docs-screenshot" />

- **Always in sync:** Built directly on your data catalog metadata, so any
  schema or catalog change updates the UI immediately.
- **Production-ready UX:** Fully responsive, internationalizable (i18n), and
  accessibility-optimized out of the box.
- **Open-source & customizable:** Self-host the app from the
  [semantius-app](https://github.com/semantius/semantius-app) repository to
  brand and customize it completely. It works with both
  [Semantius Cloud](https://app.semantius.com) and a
  [self-hosted](/docs/self-hosted) Semantius backend.
