---
title: "Data Catalog"
description: "The core of Semantius: modules, entities and fields, permissions and roles, business logic and audit logs, kept as data and enforced inside the database."
---

The data catalog is the core of Semantius. It describes your business as
data: the [modules](/docs/modules) that group the parts of one application,
the [entities and fields](/docs/entities) that become PostgreSQL tables with
an API, the [permissions and roles](/docs/permissions) that decide who reads
and writes each record, the [business logic](/docs/business-logic) that
derives and checks values, and the [audit logs](/docs/audit-logs) that record
every change. A change to an entity or a field is applied to its table in the
same transaction, so the model and the database cannot drift apart.

The data catalog is part of every edition of Semantius.
[Semantius Cloud](https://app.semantius.com) runs it for you,
[Semantius Open Source](/docs/self-hosted) runs it on your own machine or
server, and the [pg_semantius](/docs/pg-semantius) extension adds it to a
PostgreSQL database you already run. In all three it is enforced inside the
database, so an agent, a script and the web app all pass through the same
checks.

## Modules

A [module](/docs/modules) groups the entities, permissions and roles of one
application, such as a CRM or a helpdesk. Its
[version](/docs/modules#version) counts the changes to its model, and
[deleting it](/docs/modules#deleting-a-module) removes its entities with
their records, its permissions and its roles.

## Entities and Fields

An [entity](/docs/entities) is a kind of record, such as a ticket or an
order. Saving one creates its table, its API endpoint and its access
policies. Each [field](/docs/entities#fields) is a column of that table, and
its [format](/docs/entities#formats) decides what the database accepts.
[Relationships](/docs/entities#relationships) link records to each other, and
[subtypes and extensions](/docs/entities#subtypes-and-extensions) share
records with a base entity.

## Permissions and Roles

[Permissions and roles](/docs/permissions) decide who may read and write each
entity. Each entity names a view permission and an edit permission,
[roles](/docs/permissions#roles) bundle permissions for users, and the
[hierarchy](/docs/permissions#the-hierarchy) lets one permission include
others. Row-level security checks them on every request.

## Business Logic

[Business logic](/docs/business-logic) is stored on entities and fields as
[JsonLogic](/docs/business-logic/jsonlogic) rules: computed fields,
validation rules and row-level select rules, which the database enforces on
every write and read, and field input modes, which forms apply as you edit.
The [JsonLogic extensions](/docs/business-logic/extensions) add the variables
and operators Semantius provides, and
[rollup fields](/docs/business-logic/rollups) aggregate the records of a
child entity.

## Audit Logs

The [audit logs](/docs/audit-logs) record every change to the records of
audited entities, and every schema change, in two append-only logs that only
administrators can read.
