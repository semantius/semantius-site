## Free Plan Until January 2027

Our billing system is still in progress, and we plan to launch paid plans in January 2027. Until then, the free plan includes 250 GB-hours of usage per month at no cost. [Sign up for the free plan](https://app.semantius.com/auth/sign-up) to get started.

## What to Expect During Early Access

Alongside our billing system, we are actively polishing the website and completing our documentation before paid plans launch in January 2027. If anything is unclear or you have questions along the way, please reach out. We're here to help you get set up.

## Semantius Open Source

Semantius Open Source is also available: the complete Semantius data platform, free and MIT-licensed, running on hardware you control. Read the [self-hosting docs](/docs/self-hosted) to get started, or explore the [`semantius-self-hosted`](https://github.com/semantius/semantius-self-hosted) repository.

## The PostgreSQL Extension

You can also install `pg_semantius`, the PostgreSQL extension at the core of Semantius, directly into your own PostgreSQL database. Check out the [`pg_semantius` docs](/docs/pg-semantius), or view the source code in the [`semantius/semantius`](https://github.com/semantius/semantius) repository.
